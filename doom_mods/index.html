Addon Spawner Helper | Version 1.4 (last updated 17 October 2026)

Download link (If you get asked about a decryption key, it's zB4RRR5IYhxhSXjjag8Izg)

A small utility mod meant for add-ons to add new weapons to Insurrection's random spawners without resorting to overrides and load order shenanigans. Currently used by Gunlocker and L-5625 Arsenal.

Arsenal | Version 1.4 (last updated 17 October 2026)

Download link (If you get asked about a decryption key, it's OnTnNvc6es_o1H-8AMenpA)

Make sure to also download the Addon Spawner Helper

  • Classic Pistol : Modernized rendition of the Beretta lookalike. (Spawns on Dead Marines)
  • Laser Pistolette : Weak but accurate alternative to the Plasma Handgun. (Spawns on Dead Marines and can drop from Laser Rifle Zombies)
  • Fragshot : Pump action explosive slugger. (Can replace Shotguns and SSGs after gamelevel 20)
  • Chaos Phalanx : 2 rounds of charged magnesium slugs unstable chaos bolts per trigger pull. (Can replace Rocket Launchers after gamelevel 35)
  • Hellshot : It's like that gun in Doom 2016. (Can replace Plasmaguns after gamelevel 25)

Added in v1.1

  • Red Nine : A beast of a pistol with a debilitating reload time. (Spawns on Dead Marines and rarely replaces Assault Rifles)
  • Doomstick : A fragile yet devastating explosive boomstick. (Durability weapon. Can rarely replace SSGs after gamelevel 40)
  • Mag Shotgun : What if the Riot Shotgun actually rewarded you for rapid-firing? (Can replace Shotguns and SSGs after gamelevel 30 (and can drop from HEP Executors if you have More Bosses installed))
  • Cowboy Repeater : Lever-action rifle with random crits enabled. (Can replace Assault Rifles after gamelevel 10)
  • Vulkan : A Dark Blaster in shotgun form. Extremely effective at clearing crowds. (Can replace SSGs and Plasmaguns after gamelevel 50)

Added in v1.2

  • Nail Pistol : Diminutive cousin of the Nailgun, if you couldn't figure that one out. (Spawns on Dead Marines)
  • Superheavy Shotgun : The hateful child of an SSG and an ASG. (Can replace SSGs after gamelevel 40)
  • Wildfire : The long-awaited Cinder Zombie SMG that never got into Gunlocker. (Can replace Assault Rifles after gamelevel 25 and can drop from Cinder Zombies)
  • Tesla Launcher : The polar opposite to the Direct Hit with plasma flavoring. (Can replace Rocket Launchers after gamelevel 35)
  • Event Horizon : Finally some payback against those pesky Illusionists. (Can replace BFGs after gamelevel 40 and can very rarely drop from Illusionists)

Added in v1.3

  • Scientist class : Starts with a Laser Pistolette and a random assortment of augments, but not particularly good with ballistic firearms.
  • Ripsaw : A little chainsaw for your fist. (Can replace Chainsaws)
  • Auger Shotgun : What if the Railgun was a double-barrel? (Can replace SSGs after gamelevel 30)
  • PDW : The lead spitter for marines on a bullet budget. (Can replace Assault Rifles after gamelevel 10)
  • Hostility FMG : Input shotgun shells and output loads of fusion plasma. (Can replace Plasmaguns after gamelevel 50)
  • Dino Hunter : A really big fucking gun. (Can replace BFGs after gamelevel 25)

Added in v1.4

  • Engineer class : Starts with a Nail Pistol and a recharging Armor Reconstruction Kit.
  • Classic Shotgun : Wood furniture. Seven pellets. No vertical spread. Just the way you remember it. (Can replace Shotguns)
