L-5625

My Pandemonia Insurrection add-ons, adding more weapons and some extra stuff.

Insurrection Addon Helper | Version 2.1 (last updated 14 November 2026)

Download link (If you get asked about a decryption key, it's zB4RRR5IYhxhSXjjag8Izg)

A small utility mod meant for add-ons to add new weapons to Insurrection's random spawners without resorting to overrides and load order shenanigans. Currently used by Gunlocker and L-5625 Arsenal+Surplus.

Since v2.0, also serves as a library for other mods to add their own Gunsmithing recipes and is required for every L-5625.
